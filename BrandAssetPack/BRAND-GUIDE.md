# OpenDating Brand Asset Pack

## Brand character
**Warm · Clean · Trustworthy**

OpenDating should feel like a quiet coffee shop: calm, safe, human, modern, and gender-neutral.

## Mark
The selected direction is an **abstract OD monogram** built from soft continuous circles and a rounded `d` stem. It intentionally avoids hearts, flames, roses, faces, and dating-app clichés.

### App icon
- Background: `#E8735A`
- Mark: `#FAF9F7`
- No gradient
- No text
- Mark remains inside the central safe area and is designed to survive 20px rendering.

## Typography
No fonts are bundled.
- iOS: SF Pro Display / SF Pro Text
- Android: Google Sans / Roboto
- Web fallback: Inter, system-ui, sans-serif
- Weights: 400 / 500 / 600 / 700

## Wordmark
Sentence case: **OpenDating**.
Use coral on light backgrounds and `#F0856E` on dark backgrounds.

## Illustration
Warm line art, organic forms, warm gray + coral, two or three colors maximum. Avoid clip art, emoji-as-art, or dating clichés.
