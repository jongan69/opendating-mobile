OpenDating is not a drop-in Bumble replacement. Bumble is an available iOS and Android product with an established account, safety, support, and paid-feature system. OpenDating is useful only to testers who specifically want a no-phone account, approximate-area discovery, and end-to-end encrypted message content.

## Verification and account recovery

Bumble's current privacy policy says a phone number is required for account verification and its one-member-one-account approach. It may require photo or identity verification in some cases; that is not the same as every member being identity verified.

OpenDating does not require a phone number or email address. Its recovery-key model has no conventional password reset and remains a pre-release security limitation. People who value support-assisted recovery may prefer Bumble's conventional account model.

## Location and messages

Bumble's policy says that, with permission, it can collect Wi-Fi access-point information and longitude and latitude, and it presents general location information to other members. It also says location permission can be disabled.

OpenDating converts coordinates on-device to a geohash prefix of at most five characters. The approximate area and network metadata still reach OpenDating-operated services; coarse location is not anonymity.

Bumble's policy says it analyzes message content for topics, sentiment, and trends after steps intended to remove identifying information, and that moderation providers may process reports, messages, and media. OpenDating encrypts direct-message content end to end, while still allowing a member to intentionally send selected evidence to moderation. No claim here implies Bumble employees read every message.

## Pricing and platform support

Bumble is free to download and use on iOS and Android, with optional Boost, Premium, Premium+, Spotlight, SuperSwipe, and Notes products. Bumble says prices vary by tier, duration, and package and directs members to the in-app Pay Plan page. Bumble Web was discontinued in March 2026.

OpenDating targets iOS and Android but has no generally available store release. Its free core and optional one-time theme purchase remain part of a blocked release candidate.

## Who should choose Bumble instead

Choose Bumble for a live member network, current store distribution, conventional recovery and support, or optional paid discovery tools. Consider OpenDating only for testing when its narrower data inputs and encrypted message content justify its recovery, availability, network, and first-party-operator limits.
