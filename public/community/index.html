Community Standards

Updated August 9, 2026

OpenDating is for consenting adults seeking genuine relationships. These standards apply to profiles, reactions, messages, voice notes, reports, and conduct connected to the service.

Authenticity

Use accurate age and identity information. No impersonation, deceptive profiles, coordinated account farms, manipulated verification, or romance and financial scams.

Consent and sexual safety

No sexual content involving minors, grooming, exploitation, coercion, trafficking, non-consensual intimate imagery, unwanted explicit content, or threats to expose private material. Suspected child sexual abuse material is escalated and reported as required by law.

Respect

No harassment, stalking, threats, hate, dehumanizing content, or targeted abuse. Respect blocks, unmatches, boundaries, and requests to stop contact.

Platform integrity

No spam, scraping, automated engagement, malicious links, credential theft, attempts to evade enforcement, or attacks on the service.

Enforcement and appeals

Responses may include content rejection, warnings, limited reach, suspension, or removal. High-impact automated signals receive human review where practical, and eligible decisions may be appealed.

Report

Use in-app reporting when possible. For urgent abuse concerns email jonny2298@live.com. If anyone is in immediate danger, contact local emergency services.
