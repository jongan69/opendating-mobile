Correction: Dating App Ownership and Privacy Comparisons

August 2026

An earlier version of this article incorrectly said Tinder, Bumble, and Hinge were all owned by Match Group and presented unsupported feature comparisons as settled facts. The article has been withdrawn rather than leaving marketing claims online without current sourcing and legal review.

Correct ownership

Match Group's official materials list Tinder and Hinge in its brand portfolio. Bumble Inc.'s investor site identifies Bumble Inc. as the parent company of Bumble, Badoo, and BFF. Bumble is not owned by Match Group.

What replaces the comparison

OpenDating will publish only dated, directly sourced comparisons that distinguish app behavior, company policy, and inference. Until counsel reviews that work, the site describes OpenDating's own current implementation and measurable release gates without scoring competitors.
