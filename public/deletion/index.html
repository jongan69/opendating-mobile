Delete your account

Current method: Signed-in members can request deletion from Settings in the web or native app.

In the app

  1. Open Profile, then Settings.
  2. Choose Delete Account.
  3. Review the consequences and confirm.

The app immediately requests account deletion and clears local app state after the service confirms. Do not remove the app first if you still have access.

Cannot access the app?

Email jonny2298@live.com. Support-assisted deletion is not automatic and may require proof tied to the account. Never email a recovery key.

Retention

Profile, discovery, relationship, and delivery data are scheduled for removal. Safety records may be retained when required by law or needed to prevent repeat abuse. See the retention schedule.
