Privacy Policy

Last updated: August 7, 2026

Our Approach to Privacy

OpenDating is designed so that we cannot collect most of the data that traditional dating apps collect. Our architecture makes surveillance impossible by design — not just by policy.

What We Collect

We collect essentially nothing. OpenDating does not require an email address, phone number, or any personal identifier. Your account is a cryptographic keypair generated on your device.

Information you provide

  • Profile data — Display name, age range, gender, bio, interests, relationship intent, and photos. This is what you choose to share on your dating profile.
  • Coarse location — A 5-character geohash prefix (~5 km precision). Your exact GPS coordinates are converted to this coarse representation entirely on your device and then discarded. We never receive your precise location.
  • Messages — End-to-end encrypted. We cannot read them. Message content is encrypted with NIP-44 before transmission and can only be decrypted by the intended recipient.

Information we do NOT collect

  • Email address
  • Phone number
  • Real name
  • Precise GPS location
  • Contact lists or address books
  • Browsing history
  • Device advertising identifiers
  • Payment information (the app is free)
  • Any data from other apps on your device

How We Use Information

The limited information shared with the relay is used exclusively to provide the dating service:

  • Your coarse location enables discovery of people in your general area
  • Your profile is shown to other users in discovery (according to your visibility settings)
  • Encrypted likes are routed to the matcher service to create matches
  • Encrypted messages are routed between matched users

We do not use your data for advertising, analytics, machine learning, or any purpose other than providing the dating service.

Data Storage and Security

  • Your private key is stored exclusively in your device's secure storage (iOS Keychain / Android Keystore). It never leaves your device.
  • Messages are end-to-end encrypted using NIP-44. The relay stores only encrypted ciphertext.
  • Likes are private — encrypted as NIP-59 gift wraps to the matcher service.
  • Blocks and reports are private — encrypted to their respective services.
  • The relay operates on Cloudflare's infrastructure. Encrypted data may be cached at Cloudflare edge locations for performance.

Data Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your data with third parties. We do not have advertising partners. We do not use analytics services. There are no third-party SDKs in the app that collect data.

Your Control

  • Pause discovery — Hide your profile from new discovery at any time while keeping existing matches.
  • Delete account — Permanently removes your presence from the relay. Your keypair remains yours.
  • Export identity — Your keypair is portable. Take it to any OpenDating-compatible client.
  • Location permission — You can deny or revoke at any time. Discovery requires coarse location.

Children's Privacy

OpenDating is not intended for anyone under 18. We do not knowingly collect information from minors.

Changes to This Policy

We will update this policy if our practices change. Because our architecture prevents data collection by design, material changes are unlikely.
