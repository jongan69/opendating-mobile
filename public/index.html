Why OpenDating

A dating app that respects you — not your data.

🔑

Own Your Identity

Your account is a cryptographic keypair stored only on your device. Move it to any OpenDating client. No company can lock you out or delete your profile.

📍

Location Privacy

Only your general area (~5 km) is shared. Your exact GPS coordinates never leave your phone. Not to us, not to advertisers, not to anyone.

🔒

Encrypted Messages

All conversations are end-to-end encrypted with NIP-44. Nobody — not even the relay operator — can read your messages.

🛡️

Private Safety

Likes are private — the other person only knows if you match. Blocks happen instantly and are never announced. Reports are encrypted to moderation.

📱

Native Experience

Tinder-quality swipe gestures, photo-first cards, and smooth animations. iOS feels like SwiftUI. Android feels like Material 3.

🌐

Built on Nostr

Runs on a decentralized protocol with no central server. Your data isn't in a corporate database — it's encrypted events on a censorship-resistant relay network.

Privacy by Design

Every architectural decision was made to protect you — not to monetize you.

🔐 What OpenDating never sees

✓Your exact location — only a ~5 km geohash prefix is sent
✓Your messages — end-to-end encrypted, not stored in plaintext
✓Your private key — lives only in your device's secure storage
✓Who you like — one-way likes are encrypted gift wraps
✓Who you block — blocks are private and enforced locally first
✓Your report details — encrypted to the moderation service
